## 4. Shuffled-CoT Accuracy Finding (Tier 1, Item 4)

* **Released CSV Source:** esults/e9_shuffled_cot_Qwen2.5-0.5B.csv* **Audited Data:** Shuffled-CoT accuracy = 27.3% (N=167) vs. Structured-CoT = 22.8% on Qwen2.5-0.5B (p = 0.35, binomial difference within noise).
* **Prior Doc Discrepancy:** Claimed 'accuracy is sensitive to step order; logical coherence dictates accuracy' despite shuffled being numerically higher.
* **Audited Correction:** 'Accuracy is not reliably improved by coherent step ordering at 0.5B scale (difference within small-sample noise), confirming that small models do not reliably perform sequential deduction.'

## 5. Overclaims Softened & Key Theoretical Syntheses (Tiers 2 & 3)

1. **Scaling Law Fit (E10):** Log-linear fit yields R² = 0.018. Claim softened from 'discontinuous changepoint' to 'ΔECE does not follow a smooth scaling law; the relationship is non-linear and family-dependent.'
2. **Probing Scope:** Clarified as **5 model scales** (13,600 trajectories across 680 questions × 4 modes × 5 models), while behavioral evaluations span 11 models.
3. **Scratchpad Framing:** Re-framed sub-2B scratchpad parse collapse as an **emission / syntactic termination failure** (unclosed <scratchpad> tags), uniting it under the broader emission bottleneck thesis.
4. **Intellectual Core Synthesis (Murphy Decomposition + Probing):**
   > *While verbalized confidence exhibits near-zero Resolution (Δ = +0.0005, p=0.82), intermediate layer representations achieve high linear probe AUROC (0.68 - 0.83). The model internally possesses the discriminatory resolution that its verbalized expression entirely lacks.*
5. **CRU and Transfer Link:** Length-regime models (CRU ≤ 0) rely on shallow, non-transferring features; reasoning-regime models (≥ 3B, Scratchpad) form abstract, domain-general representations of solution correctness.
